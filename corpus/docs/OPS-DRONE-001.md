# Drone Operations Procedure
> Synthetic demo document, not a real company document.

## 1. Scope
1.1 This procedure applies to all drone (UAV) filming on Sadaf Productions sets.
1.2 It complements the Set Safety Guide (OPS-SAFETY-001). Drones may only be flown by a licensed pilot holding a valid GACA permit for the shoot, as required by that guide.

## 2. Flight Limits
2.1 Maximum altitude is 120 m above ground level.
2.2 No flights over crowds, public gatherings or unprotected members of the public.
2.3 Flights over cast and crew are limited to what the shot requires, and only after a briefing by the pilot.
2.4 The pilot keeps visual line of sight with the drone at all times.

## 3. Pre-Flight Checklist
3.1 Before every flight the pilot completes the pre-flight checklist, covering:
- permit and location boundaries confirmed;
- airframe, propellers and gimbal inspected;
- batteries charged and checked;
- GPS lock and return-to-home point set;
- weather and wind checked;
- take-off and landing zone cleared and marked.
3.2 The safety officer signs the checklist before the first flight of the day.

## 4. Battery Management
4.1 Every battery is numbered and logged.
4.2 The battery log records charge cycles, flight time and any swelling or damage.
4.3 Damaged or swollen batteries are removed from service immediately and returned to the equipment store.

## 5. Incidents
5.1 Any crash, fly-away or near miss is reported to the safety officer at once and to safety@sadaf.example in line with the Set Safety Guide.
5.2 The drone is grounded until the cause is understood.
