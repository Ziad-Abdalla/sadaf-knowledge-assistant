# Freelancer Onboarding Guide
> Synthetic demo document, not a real company document.

## 1. Purpose and Scope
1.1 This guide describes how freelance crew members (camera, lighting, sound, art department, make-up and similar roles) are onboarded before working on a Sadaf Productions series.
1.2 The guide is addressed to freelancers and to the production coordinators who bring them in.
1.3 A freelancer may not start work on set until onboarding is complete as described in section 4.

## 2. Required Documents
2.1 Each freelancer must submit the following documents to the production coordinator:
- a copy of the national ID (for Saudi nationals) or a valid iqama (for residents);
- an IBAN certificate issued by a Saudi bank, in the freelancer's own name;
- the company NDA, signed and dated.
2.2 Documents are submitted as PDF files by email to the production coordinator, who forwards them to HR for verification.
2.3 Incomplete or unreadable documents will be returned, and onboarding will not proceed until they are corrected.

## 3. Vendor Record in Odoo
3.1 Once HR has verified the documents, the finance team creates a vendor record for the freelancer in Odoo.
3.2 The vendor record holds the freelancer's legal name, ID number and bank details. Freelancers cannot edit their bank details directly; changes must be requested from finance with a new IBAN certificate.
3.3 The production coordinator does not create or modify vendor records.

## 4. Completion of Onboarding and First Payment
4.1 Onboarding is complete when the documents are verified, the NDA is filed and the Odoo vendor record is active.
4.2 The first payment to a freelancer is made only after onboarding is complete. Work performed before that point is paid once onboarding is finished.
4.3 Payment terms are set out in the freelancer's crew contract.

## 5. System Access
5.1 Freelancers receive Odoo portal access only. The portal lets them view their own purchase orders, invoices and payment status.
5.2 Freelancers do not receive internal Odoo user accounts or access to internal applications such as Inventory, Purchase or Time Off.
5.3 Portal credentials are personal and must not be shared.

## 6. Contacts
6.1 Questions about documents: the production coordinator of the series.
6.2 Questions about payments and vendor records: the finance team.
